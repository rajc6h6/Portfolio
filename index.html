A document Q&A system that cites exactly where each answer came from — and tells you what questions your knowledge base can't answer yet.

Document ingestion → chunking + embedding → pgvector store → user query → hybrid retrieval → answer generation with inline citations → gap detection (unanswered queries logged) → periodic content gap report

> Answers grounded in source documents with paragraph-level citations. Content gap report surfaced knowledge base blind spots the client didn't know existed.

  • ->paragraph-level citations in every response — answer is traceable to specific source sections, not just document titles
  • ->unanswered or low-confidence queries logged automatically and surfaced as a content gap report
  • ->hybrid retrieval (semantic + keyword) reduces missed answers from vocabulary mismatch
  • ->ingestion pipeline handles PDF, DOCX, and plain text without per-format custom logic

Client delivery Sales automation

AI Lead Classifier & CRM Automation with Smart Escalation

Scores and routes inbound leads automatically — high-value ones go straight to CRM with enriched context, the rest are filtered before they waste anyone's time.

Inbound lead data → AI scoring model → tier classification → high-tier: enrich + push to CRM + notify sales → low-tier: auto-filter or nurture sequence → escalation rules for edge cases

> Sales team receives pre-qualified, enriched leads. Manual lead review time cut significantly. Escalation rules ensure no high-value lead is misclassified without a human check.

  • ->multi-factor scoring: firmographic signals, intent indicators, and source quality combined, not just a single field match
  • ->CRM push includes enriched context (company size, inferred intent, lead source) — not just raw form data
  • ->smart escalation: leads near the classification boundary are flagged for human review rather than silently dropped or promoted
  • ->audit log for every classification decision so the sales team can retrain the scoring rules from real feedback
